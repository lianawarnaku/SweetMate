# App Store submission preparation

Use the next explicitly authorized and accepted native candidate. This file does
not authorize a build, purchase, TestFlight distribution or public submission.

Public URLs are live: homepage https://sweetmate.info, privacy
https://sweetmate.info/privacy, support https://sweetmate.info/support, account
deletion instructions https://sweetmate.info/delete-account.

Suggested description opening: SweetMate brings shared-home chores, shopping
lists, expenses and borrowing together. Create a household, review a recurring
chore chart with your Sweetmates, and keep track of shared responsibilities.
Optionally connect Google Calendar and Tasks to view your schedule beside chores.

Review items before final submission:

- Reconcile privacy labels against the actual candidate and vendor settings.
  Product/crash consent is optional; item-name analytics stays disabled. No GPS
  location is collected. Manual Home status describes user-shared presence.
- Reuse the dedicated dummy-household reviewer account already prepared for
  Apple; verify its login on the candidate. Keep passwords in Apple's private
  fields/private storage, never this repository. Confirm notes explain the app
  can be reviewed without connecting a personal Google account.
- Capture genuine device screenshots for My Sweet, household chores/chart,
  shopping, expenses/IOUs, borrowing and settings. Do not use browser personas
  as proof that the native candidate works.
- Confirm name/subtitle, description, keywords, category, age rating, support and
  privacy URLs, ownership, territories, availability and manual-release choice.
  The owner described this as a noncommercial hobby; do not change the trader
  declaration based on a hypothetical business model.
- Check the exact candidate's SDK/build, encryption/export-compliance answers,
  Apple sign-in and in-app account deletion, plus production service availability.
- Finish the phone acceptance sheet, Google public-access review and remaining
  vendor retention/erasure checks. Submit only truthful tested behavior.

Account-owner sign-in/2FA may be required to inspect or save App Store Connect.
No store metadata or public-release status changed during this preparation.
